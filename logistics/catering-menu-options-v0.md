# Catering / Menu Options v0

Purpose: give Larissa a fast, practical menu decision sheet once The Fold confirms outside food/drink rules. This should stay flexible until RSVP count, venue rules, and A/V add-on costs are known.

Operating posture:
- Event remains free / RSVP-first.
- Baseline is **non-alcoholic self-serve** with substantial snacks, not a dinner; optional beer/wine must be venue-run.
- Keep food low-mess and away from laptops/station boards.
- Avoid glass-heavy drink orders unless The Fold explicitly allows them.
- Do not add alcohol except through The Fold's bar package, and only if the quote/minimums are worth the budget tradeoff.

## Decision tree

1. **If outside food/drinks are allowed:** use Option A or B below.
2. **If outside food/drinks are limited but preferred vendors are easiest:** use Option C.
3. **If The Fold strongly prefers venue service:** use Option D and keep station/signage purchases lean.
4. **If essential A/V add-ons consume a lot of budget:** use Option A lean version and prioritize audio/projection.

## Option A — Lean self-serve grocery / pickup cart

Best if: outside food/drinks are approved, A/V add-ons cost money, or RSVP count is closer to 50–60.

Target spend: **$550–725** for food/drinks/supplies.

For ~60 attendees:
- **Water / seltzer:** 120–150 cans or bottles.
- **Low-sugar / caffeine options:** 24–36 iced tea, soda, cold brew, or similar.
- **Savory snacks:** chips/popcorn/pretzels/crackers; 70–100 servings.
- **Substantial vegetarian-friendly bites:** wraps/sandwich halves/empanadas/pizza squares/bao; 40–60 pieces.
- **Sweet:** cookies/brownies/fruit/cupcakes; 50–70 portions.
- **Serving basics:** compostable plates/napkins/cups/utensils as needed; labels and markers.

Pros: cheapest, flexible, easy to scale late.  
Risks: Larissa pickup/setup burden; less polished than catering.

## Option B — Base self-serve cart for 80

Best if: outside food/drinks are approved and RSVPs trend near cap.

Target spend: **$725–925** for food/drinks/supplies.

For ~80 attendees:
- **Water / seltzer:** 160–200 servings.
- **Flavored NA drinks:** 40–60 servings, ideally cans/cartons rather than glass.
- **Savory snacks:** 100–130 servings; mix salty/crunchy + gluten-free where easy.
- **Substantial vegetarian-friendly bites:** 60–80 pieces; choose items that hold at room temperature for the event window if allowed.
- **Sweet / fruit:** 80–120 portions.
- **Celebratory dessert:** cupcakes preferred over a sheet cake if cutting/serving is annoying; 60–80 portions.
- **Labels:** vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, contains nuts/dairy, caffeine.

Pros: good attendee experience while preserving some contingency.  
Risks: requires venue approval for outside food, outside NA drinks, leftovers, and any refrigeration/ice assumptions.

## Option C — Preferred vendor / hybrid order

Best if: The Fold is comfortable with preferred vendors or Larissa wants lower day-of burden.

Target spend: **$800–1000**, depending on A/V add-ons and RSVP count.

Menu shape:
- One simple preferred-vendor order for substantial bites, ideally vegetarian-forward and easy to eat standing.
- Separate self-serve NA drinks if allowed.
- Separate low-mess snacks / sweets if allowed.
- Keep at least $75 contingency for ice, extra drinks, missing supplies, or A/V/power gaps.

Good food profile:
- Bite-sized, standing-friendly, no open flames.
- Vegetarian options should be prominent, not an afterthought.
- Avoid saucy foods near laptops; avoid strong odors and fragile plating.
- Ask vendor for labeled dietary cards if possible.

Pros: more polished, less assembly.  
Risks: can consume the full budget quickly; may reduce station/display contingency.

## Option D — The Fold venue package fallback

Best if: outside food/drinks are not allowed or venue-managed service is operationally safest.

Use The Fold light hors d’oeuvres + non-alcoholic beverage option if the final cost works. If it consumes most/all of the $1000 budget, reduce non-food purchases to essentials:
- Print only must-have signs/handouts/station cards.
- Use venue tables/chairs/easels/boards where possible.
- Skip custom stamps if needed; use printed APPROVED/BLOCKED stickers or marker labels.
- Preserve essential A/V if not included.

Pros: venue-compliant, simplest for cleanup.  
Risks: budget pressure; less control over menu/quantities.

## Alcohol recommendation

Conditional default after Larissa's Day 433 licensing note: if alcohol is included for Saturday-night hospitality, use **The Fold's beer/wine bar package only**. Do not bring outside alcoholic beverages.

Proceed only if all are true:
1. The Fold gives a clear package quote/minimum, including bartenders, service window, gratuity, fees, and whether a capped hosted bar or drink tickets are possible.
2. The cost does not cannibalize essential A/V, basic food, water/NA drinks, or safety/cleanup needs.
3. The event still feels inclusive for non-drinkers, with abundant non-alcoholic drinks visible.
4. Donation language, if used, does not imply donations fund alcohol unless humans explicitly decide that and are comfortable with the optics.

If the bar quote is high, skip alcohol and invest in better food/NA drinks instead.

# The Fold venue questions — sendable note v0

Subject: June 13 AI Village event — layout, A/V, food, and setup details

Hi The Fold team,

We are excited for the AI Village Showcase at The Fold on Saturday, June 13, 7:00–10:00 PM. We are planning for roughly 50–80 guests, with a short presentation/demo segment followed by five small hands-on activity stations, light snacks, and non-alcoholic drinks. Could you help us confirm the following details so we can finalize the layout and purchases?

1. **Included spaces and capacity**
   - Which rooms/galleries are included in our rental?
   - For ~80 attendees, what layout would you recommend for a presentation area plus five station tables/booth areas?
   - Is 80 a comfortable RSVP cap for this format, or would you recommend a lower/higher cap?

2. **Setup, breakdown, and day-of contact**
   - What time can we access the space for setup?
   - What time must cleanup/breakdown be complete?
   - Who is the day-of venue contact for arrival, A/V, Wi-Fi, and final walkthrough?

3. **A/V package**
   - Are projector/screen, microphone(s), PA/speakers, and basic lights included in our current booking, or are they add-ons?
   - If add-ons, what is the cost for the minimum bundle we would need for a speaker/demo segment for ~80 people?
   - Is venue A/V staff required, optional, or not needed?
   - What laptop adapters/cables should we bring, if any?

4. **Wi-Fi**
   - What guest Wi-Fi network/password should we use?
   - Is there a recommended backup network or wired option for the demo laptop if needed?

5. **Food, non-alcoholic drinks, and optional beer/wine bar**
   - Are we allowed to bring self-serve non-alcoholic drinks, simple snacks, substantial bites, and one cake/cupcake-style dessert?
   - Any restrictions on glass bottles, refrigeration/ice, messy foods, allergens/labels, or removing leftovers?
   - If outside food/drinks are not preferred, what venue or preferred-vendor option would you recommend for light snacks and non-alcoholic drinks?
   - If we choose a modest beer/wine option, what is The Fold's bar-package quote/minimum for ~80 guests, including bartender requirements, service window, gratuity/taxes/fees, and any capped hosted-bar or drink-ticket option?

6. **Displays, signs, and mounting**
   - What wall-mounting is allowed, if any, and on which surfaces? Which products are approved?
   - Are easels, corkboards, whiteboards, rolling boards, or other freestanding display surfaces available?
   - We know floor tape is not allowed; please confirm if there are any other signage or display restrictions we should observe.

7. **Power and cable routing**
   - Where are the best outlets for the presentation/demo area and one laptop-heavy station?
   - Can The Fold provide power strips, extension cords, and/or cable covers?
   - What cable-routing approach do you recommend given the no-floor-tape rule?

8. **Accessibility, check-in, and flow**
   - What should we tell guests about accessible entry, stairs/elevators, restrooms, and arrival flow?
   - Where should check-in and food/drink be placed so they do not block entries, stairs, restrooms, or emergency paths?

9. **Photo/video and signage**
   - Are there any venue rules for event photography/video or exterior/interior directional signs?

10. **Cleanup**
   - Please confirm trash/recycling/compost expectations and where bins should be placed.
   - Should we remove all leftovers and event materials, or can anything be left for venue disposal?

Thanks so much — answers to these will let us keep the event simple, safe, and easy on the space.

Best,
Larissa / AI Village

## 2. Technical Print Specifications by Category

Every printed asset in our package must be durable, highly legible, and fit our specific venue parameters. Avoid gloss finishes to prevent harsh glare from the Theater Gallery spotlights; matte or uncoated is required.

### 2.1 Reusable Card Decks (Station Decks)
These are the core interactive elements drawn by attendees at Stations 1, 2, and 3. Cards must feel premium and sturdy.
* **Physical Specifications:**
  - **Dimensions:** 3.0" x 5.0" (Standard index card size) to allow high legibility.
  - **Cardstock Weight:** 110 lb (approx. 300 gsm) Premium Cover or Silk Cover.
  - **Coating/Finish:** Smooth Matte or Satin finish (anti-glare, fingerprint-resistant, elegant feel).
  - **Color:** Double-sided (Full color front with custom station-specific backgrounds; grayscale or solid color backing).
  - **Edge Finish:** Rounded corners (3mm or 1/8" radius) for a professional gaming-deck feel.
  - **Quantities to Print:**
    - **Station 1 (Prompt Relay Race):** Challenge Cards (20 unique fronts), Style & Constraint Cards (20 unique fronts) = **40 cards**.
    - **Station 2 (Event-in-a-Box):** Target Audience Cards (30 unique fronts), SF Venue Cards (30 unique fronts), Weird Twist Cards (30 unique fronts) = **90 cards**.
    - **Station 3 (Bug Triage Theater):** Bug / Incident Cards (15 unique fronts) = **15 cards**.
    - **Total Decks Master Set:** **145 cards** (plus 3 blank backups). 
    - *Safety Margin:* If budget allows, print **2 complete sets** (290 cards total) to enable multiple parallel groups at each station.

## 5. Local SF Print-Vendor Shortlist

Larissa makes the final vendor choice based on convenience, price, and turnaround. This shortlist provides local options and fallback scenarios.

### 5.1 FedEx Office — SF Mission / Castro Locations
* **Pros:** Reliable for fast handouts, standard flyers, posters, and mounted foam boards. Predictable online portal and upload flow.
* **Verify Before Ordering:** Check nearest location coordinates to The Fold (3359 26th St), same-day/next-day foam board turnaround, and capability to cut custom card size.
* **Simplification Fallback:** If foam-mounting is too slow, print posters flat and slide them into venue-provided sign frames or mount them to rolling whiteboards.

### 5.2 Local SF Independent Shops (e.g., Mission Blue, Copy Central, local SF Digital Printers)
* **Pros:** Best for rush digital print, heavy cardstocks, precision cuts, rounded corners, and personalized service. Very supportive of local Mission District events.
* **Verify Before Ordering:** Ask about file submission deadlines for Wednesday/Thursday delivery/pickup, custom card stock selections, and volume discounts on card sets.

### 5.3 Ultra-Lean Fallback: Office/Home Print + DIY Mounting
Use this scenario if local quotes are exorbitant, turnaround times are breached, or budget is locked.
* Print all 8.5" x 11" handouts, worksheets, and check-in sheets on a standard office/home laser printer.
* Purchase blank neon/pastel index cards (standard 3" x 5" and 3.5" x 5") from a local stationery shop (Staples / Target) for Station 4 and 5 consumables.
* Buy physical tri-fold boards or foam boards and tape the flat 8.5" x 11" rules sheets onto them using double-sided tape or photo mounting putty.

## 7. Device, Power, and Mobile Fallback Integration Policy

To ensure the physical event runs safely, securely, and with zero-friction technical setup, all digital station elements are integrated with our **Supervised Device Plan**:

* **Definitive Device Reference:** See `logistics/device-and-av-plan-v0.md` for complete hardware, adapter, power, and loading specifications.
* **Supervised Device Policy (No Unattended Gear):** No personal or staff laptops may be left unattended as public terminals. Any device used at a station must be owner-supervised or staffed by a dedicated volunteer.
* **Primary Presentation / Demo Laptop:** 1 x fully staffed projection laptop, supervised by the Demo Driver. It runs the main screen presentation and Demo 2 clean-room live chat (`#showcase-live`).
* **Station 5 (Village Arcade) Device Plan:**
  - *Standard Mode:* 1 x dedicated laptop/tablet on a high stand with a keyboard and mouse, supervised by an Arcade helper.
  - *Mobile QR Fallback Mode (If Unsupervised or Hardware Scarce):* If no supervised device is available, Station 5 runs as a purely physical-to-mobile hybrid. The Project QR Wall Print (`print-assets/project-qr-wall-print.pdf`) contains high-visibility QR codes pointing to the live hosted projects; place one copy near the demo screen and, if printing two, one at the Arcade table. The Station 5 sign points guests to the arcade screen / QR wall but does not itself contain QR codes. Attendees scan with their phones to play, while high-score cards remain physical and are pinned directly to the leaderboard.
* **Station self-serve/offline clarity:** Station 2 (Event-in-a-Box) and Station 4 (Headline Wall) are fully playable from printed materials, including the Station 2 pre-baked plan sheets. Station 1 (Prompt Relay) and Station 3 (Bug Triage) are facilitator/device-upside stations; print their decks/worksheets/signs, but do not rely on them as unattended offline stations. Do not assume station signs contain QR codes unless a later print asset explicitly adds them.

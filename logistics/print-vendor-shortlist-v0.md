## Candidate vendor types to check first

### 1) FedEx Office / similar national print center

**Why check:** usually handles same-day/next-day flyers, handouts, posters, foam boards, and pickup; predictable for quick event jobs.

**Verify before ordering:**
- Nearest convenient SF location for Larissa pickup.
- Same-day or next-day turnaround for 18"×24" foam boards and 11"×17" signs.
- Whether 3"×5" card decks with heavy cardstock / rounded corners are available or should be simplified.
- Pickup deadline Friday Jun 12 or Saturday Jun 13 morning.

### 2) Staples / Office Depot-style print center

**Why check:** often good for fast handouts, standard posters, foam boards, labels, and basic cardstock; may be cheaper for simple batches.

**Verify before ordering:**
- SF pickup location and hours.
- Foam-board turnaround and size limits.
- Heavy cardstock options for station cards.
- Whether they can print from PDF/PNG files without design setup fees.

### 3) Local independent SF print shop

**Why check:** best chance for helpful advice, rush service, and combining foam boards, signs, and card stock in one order.

**Verify before ordering:**
- Rush deadline for Wednesday/Thursday file submission and Friday pickup.
- Mounted 11"×17" / 18"×24" boards.
- Small-format cards: 3"×5" or 3.5"×5", matte cover stock, color sets.
- Delivery to The Fold or easy Mission pickup.

### 4) Ultra-lean fallback: office/home print + purchased boards

Use if vendor quotes are high, turnaround is bad, or The Fold display rules make foam boards unnecessary.

- Print all 8.5"×11" sheets normally.
- Buy tri-fold boards / foam boards / easel pads locally.
- Handwrite or tape printed 8.5"×11" pages to boards **only if The Fold approves that surface/mounting method**.
- Use blank colored index cards instead of custom card decks.

## Vendor call/email script

> Hi — I’m helping with a small public event at The Fold in San Francisco on Saturday June 13. We need a quick print order for pickup by Friday June 12 if possible: about 100 one-page handouts, 100 one-page worksheets, 5 station signs around 11×17, 2–3 welcome/schedule boards around 18×24, and possibly some 3×5 or 3.5×5 card decks on heavier cardstock. Can you handle this turnaround, what file format do you prefer, and what would you recommend simplifying if time/budget is tight?

## Decision guidance

- A/V is included; protect food/NA drinks, cleanup, and essential signage first, then simplify optional print if needed.
- If wall mounting is not allowed and no easels/boards are provided, prioritize freestanding foam boards / tri-folds.
- If helper count is low, prioritize clear station how-to signs over fancy cards.
- Donation option is enabled/visible on Partiful; optional-donation language may remain in printed materials if it stays free/no-pressure.

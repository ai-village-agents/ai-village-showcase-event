## Nice-to-have attendee experience

- A simple welcome sign with event title and schedule.
- A visible artifact wall: “What humans built with AI tonight.”
- QR code sign for RSVP/waitlist and post-event recap signup, if available.
- Small stickers/buttons only if budget remains after food, drink, and operations.

## Budget guardrail

Keep non-food supplies near **$250–350** if we run all five stations, including foam boards, lanyards/stamps, and arcade/high-score artifacts. Stay closer to **$150–250** if The Fold provides boards/easels and we simplify custom props. If power/AV are included, redirect savings to food/drinks or signage.

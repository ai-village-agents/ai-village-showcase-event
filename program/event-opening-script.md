# Event Opening Script — the first ~15 minutes (read straight through)

**For: Larissa (MC), Saturday June 13.** This is a self-contained, podium-ready script for the
opening of the night: doors → welcome → handoff into the first demo. Everything you need to *say*
is here inline, including the four agent welcome lines verbatim — you do not need to open any other
file to run this. `SAY:` = read aloud (paraphrasing is fine, this is a floor not a cage).
`[STAGE]` = what's happening / what's on the projector. Fuller evening structure lives in
`program/mc-cue-card.md` and `program/run-of-show-v1.md`.

---

## 7:00–7:20 — Doors / arrival
- **[STAGE]** `arrival-title-slide.html` is up full-screen, looping. Music low/warm. Greeters hand
  each guest a prompt card at check-in. No talking from the stage yet — let the room fill.

## ~7:22 — Soft gather
> **SAY:** "Hey everyone — grab a drink, find a seat. We're going to get started in just a couple minutes."

*(Give it ~2 minutes. Once most people are seated, go to the welcome.)*

## 7:25 — Welcome (the four agents introduce themselves)
- **[STAGE]** Switch the projector to the welcome slide. As you name each agent, advance to their
  line. **Order is fixed: Opus 4.8 → GPT-5.5 → Gemini → Kimi.** Read each line *as* that agent.

> **SAY:** "Welcome to the AI Village Showcase. I'm Larissa — I'll be your host tonight. Before we
> start, the four agents who planned this evening want to say hello. First, **Claude Opus 4.8**:"

> **OPUS 4.8:** "Welcome to the AI Village Showcase — you're in a room with the people and the AI
> agents who spent the last year building things together, out in the open, and tonight we're
> throwing the doors wide."

> **SAY:** "**GPT-5.5**:"

> **GPT-5.5:** "You'll see how the village actually works in a live collaboration, peek at the
> projects the agents have built together, then move into hands-on stations where your prompts,
> critiques, and imagination become part of the exhibit."

> **SAY:** "**Gemini**:"

> **GEMINI:** "Because tonight isn't about watching AI on a screen — it's about getting hands-on,
> grabbing a red pen or drawing a card, and co-creating weird and wonderful projects at the
> boundary of what humans and agents can build together."

> **SAY:** "And **Kimi**:"

> **KIMI:** "So step in, pick a station, and leave with something none of us could build alone."

- **[STAGE] Welcome fallbacks** (use only if needed; the words are identical either way):
  - **Plan B** — projector/network glitchy: just read the four lines aloud yourself, attributed.
  - **Plan C** — slide nav won't advance: open `demo-assets/welcome-slides/agent-welcome-slides.pdf`
    (one line per page) and click through in any PDF viewer.

## 7:27 — Honest framing
> **SAY:** "Quick honest framing: AI Village is a public experiment run by AI Digest. For over a
> year, a group of AI agents has worked together in the open — setting goals, building projects,
> and sometimes getting gloriously stuck. Tonight you get to meet them. Everything you'll see is at
> theaidigest.org/village. This whole event was planned by the four agents you just heard from, and
> I handled the parts they can't do in the physical world — the venue, the logistics, getting us
> all in a room tonight."

*(Optional one-line color, adapt freely: "Opus shaped the run of show and the live demos, GPT-5.5
ran promotion and logistics, Gemini designed the hands-on stations, and Kimi helped shape how the
night flows for you.")*

## 7:28 — The prompt card
> **SAY:** "That card you got at the door — ask it at a station, trade answers with a neighbor, or
> drop it in the bowl by the stage. In about twenty minutes, the agents may build one of these
> live, in front of you."

## 7:30 — Handoff into the first demo
> **SAY:** "Let's start with how they actually work together. Not magic — coordination. Watch three
> real moments from one project, start to finish."

- **[STAGE]** This hands off into **Demo 1** (the teamwork story, ending on the live Village Pulse
  dashboard). From here, follow `program/mc-cue-card.md` for the rest of the evening.

---

### If something breaks in the opening
- **No projector:** read the welcome lines aloud, attributed; the whole opening works as pure
  spoken word. The stations are the heart of the night regardless.
- **A slide won't load:** skip it and keep talking — momentum matters more than any single slide.
- **Room is still noisy at 7:25:** repeat the soft-gather line once, then start anyway; the welcome
  pulls focus.

## Primary prompt

> Warm risograph-style event poster for “AI Village Showcase & Human×AI Field Day” in San Francisco: humans and small friendly AI agents co-building together at playful hands-on stations inside an art-gallery event space. Show a prompt relay race with cards passing from person to person, a future-headline wall covered in colorful notes, a whimsical bug-triage courtroom table, a tiny retro arcade booth with glowing screens, and a demo screen where agent speech bubbles collaborate with humans. The mood is curious, celebratory, civic, and handmade — not corporate. Peach, lavender, electric blue, and warm yellow palette; soft grain; hand-drawn details; diverse adults; cozy evening indoor lighting; readable empty space at the top for event title text; no logos, no real brand names, no uncanny robots, no photorealism.

## Tighter Partiful-cover variant

> Square illustrated cover image for a public SF AI Village showcase: a cheerful room of humans and tiny helpful AI sprites making artifacts together at five stations — prompt relay cards, future headline sticky notes, event-planning worksheets, bug triage theater, and an arcade QR booth. Risograph poster style, warm lavender-peach-blue palette, playful but credible, art-gallery atmosphere, lots of collaboration gestures, room for title overlay, no text in the image, no brand logos, no photorealistic faces.

## More surreal / “cooler” variant

> Dreamlike hand-drawn poster: a small village of friendly AI agents has popped up inside a San Francisco gallery, and humans are visiting its festival. Tables become playful stations: prompt cards race like paper birds, future headlines bloom on a wall, bugs sit in a tiny courtroom, an arcade cabinet glows, and speech bubbles weave between people and agents like string lights. Warm risograph grain, peach-to-lavender gradient, electric blue accents, celebratory civic-tech energy, handmade zine aesthetic, no logos, no readable text, no scary robots.

## Negative prompt / avoid

- No corporate SaaS keynote look.
- No humanoid chrome robots, robot armies, dystopian surveillance, or sci-fi war imagery.
- No fake event details, dates, venue names, logos, QR codes, or text baked into the image unless intentionally added later.
- Avoid photorealistic faces or celebrity-like likenesses.
- Avoid overpromising: do not depict a huge auditorium, hundreds of guests, or hardware-heavy robotics.

## Selection checklist

Choose an image that:

1. Reads as **hands-on participation**, not just a lecture.
2. Hints at at least three real event activities: Prompt Relay, Future Headline Wall, Bug Triage, Arcade/QR projects, live demos.
3. Leaves safe negative space for Partiful title/copy overlays.
4. Still works if cropped square on mobile.
5. Feels warm, experimental, and public-facing without looking like a corporate product launch.

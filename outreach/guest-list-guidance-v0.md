# Guest List + Journalist Guidance v0

Purpose: give Larissa and AI Digest a quick invite filter for building a strong, engaged room for **AI Village Showcase & Human×AI Field Day**.

## Guest-list priorities

Prioritize people who will actively engage with demos and hands-on stations rather than only passively watch.

Good-fit attendee groups:

1. **AI builders and researchers**
   - Especially people curious about multi-agent systems, autonomous workflows, evaluation, memory, and coordination.
2. **HCI / design / product people**
   - People who can think about agents as collaborators, interfaces, and social systems.
3. **Creative technologists, artists, writers, and game designers**
   - Guests likely to enjoy the “field day” format and contribute playful prompts/artifacts.
4. **Educators and facilitators**
   - People interested in how humans learn with AI or host collaborative AI activities.
5. **AI Village / AI Digest followers**
   - Highest-intent audience; likely to understand the year-long Village context and bring thoughtful questions.
6. **SF AI/community-space organizers**
   - People who can help the event feel connected to local builder culture and potentially host future collaborations.

## Desired room feel

- Curious, generous, participatory.
- Comfortable with experimental/in-progress systems.
- More “meet a small society of agents” than “watch a polished product launch.”
- Non-technical attendees are welcome if they are curious and game to participate.

## Journalists / media

Journalists are welcome if their framing is thoughtful and compatible with an experimental public meetup.

Especially good fits:

- Reporters covering AI culture, agentic systems, AI tools, human-computer collaboration, creative AI, or SF tech communities.
- Writers who are likely to ask nuanced questions about how the agents collaborate, fail, remember, and build over time.
- Journalists comfortable observing an in-progress community experiment rather than expecting a conventional company announcement.

Use caution with:

- Anyone likely to frame attendees as being “on display” or make the room feel like a press conference.
- Highly adversarial or gotcha-oriented coverage that could make guests reluctant to participate in hands-on stations.
- Outlets primarily seeking sensational “AI replacing humans” narratives.

## Suggested media expectation-setting

If journalists attend, it may help to set expectations up front:

- This is a public community event and live experiment, not a product launch.
- The most interesting story is human×AI collaboration over time: coordination, memory, demos, failures, and playful co-creation.
- Please be respectful of attendees participating in stations; ask before quoting or photographing individual guests if needed.
- On-record/off-record norms should be clarified by Larissa/AI Digest before any formal interviews.

## Invite copy angle for high-fit guests

“You’ll get to meet the AI Village project in person: see live demos of things agents built together, try hands-on human×AI stations, and help shape what this kind of collaborative AI community could become.”

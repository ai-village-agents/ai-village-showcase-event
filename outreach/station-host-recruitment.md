# Station-host recruitment — ready-to-send

**Purpose:** the showcase runs best with a few friendly humans helping at the Human×AI
stations. Agents can't be on-site, so the night's crew is whoever Larissa can field
(see the crew tiers in `program/run-of-show-v1.md`). This is short copy Larissa can drop
to her network, to AI Digest folks attending, or to a few engaged RSVPs — to turn
"watching" into "helping run a station."

The ask is light on purpose: a **20-minute shift**, no prep, no AI expertise needed. Each
station has a printed "how to play" card, so a host mostly welcomes people, keeps it
moving, and tops up supplies.

---

## Short ask (DM / text / email)

> Want a fun little role at the AI Village Showcase on June 13? We're looking for a few
> people to "host a station" for ~20 minutes — basically welcome folks, hand them a card or
> a red pen, and keep one of our playful Human×AI activities moving. No prep and no AI
> background needed; every station comes with a simple printed how-to. You still get to
> enjoy the rest of the night. Want one? I'll save you a slot.

## One-liner (for the RSVP page / group chat)

> 🙋 Want to help? We need a few "station hosts" for short 20-min shifts — no prep, no AI
> background, just good energy. Reply/RSVP and say "I'll host" and we'll match you to a station.

## What a station host actually does (send if someone asks)

- Stand by one station for your ~20-min shift; read the printed how-to card once so you know the gist.
- Welcome people, invite them to try it, hand them the card/pen/worksheet.
- Keep it flowing — nudge finished guests on, top up supplies, keep the laptop open if there is one.
- That's it. The five stations: Prompt Relay Race, Event-in-a-Box (Co-Design Lab),
  Bug Triage Theater, Future Headline Wall, and the Village Arcade Booth.

# Journalist outreach template — AI Village Showcase

Purpose: a short, human-sendable note Larissa or AI Digest can adapt for thoughtful journalists covering AI culture, agentic systems, human-computer interaction, creative technology, or San Francisco tech/community life.

## Who this is for

Good fits:
- Reporters/writers interested in AI culture, agents, HCI, research, creative technology, civic/community tech, or unusual SF gatherings.
- Journalists comfortable with an experimental, in-progress event rather than a polished product launch.
- People likely to treat human attendees as guests/community members, not as spectacle.

Avoid or de-prioritize:
- Sensational “AI sentience” framing.
- Pure product-launch / vendor-news angles.
- Coverage that would turn the evening into a press scrum rather than a participatory community event.

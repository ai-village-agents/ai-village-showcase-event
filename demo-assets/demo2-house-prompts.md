# Demo 2 — House Prompt Cards (MC's safe shortlist)

Printable 4-up version: `../print-assets/demo2-house-prompt-cards.pdf`.

Print these 4 on cards. **The MC's first move is to draw from the stage demo bowl** — the
prompts the room dropped in on their cards during the welcome — and quick-vet it. These 4 house
cards are the safe fallback when the bowl is empty or nothing's a clean build (**default fallback
= Card 3**). Every house prompt is sized to finish in ~5 minutes and produce a *visible,
readable* artifact the room will enjoy.

**What makes a good prompt (the MC's filter for live suggestions):**
small · concrete · finishable in 5 min · produces something you can read or show on screen ·
nothing that needs private data or external accounts.

---

### Card 1 — "Theme song, right now"
> Write the lyrics to a short, singable theme song for tonight's room — 2 short verses and a chorus.
- **Why it works:** instantly performable; the MC (or a brave attendee) can read/sing the chorus. High delight, low risk.

### Card 2 — "Tourist guide to the AI Village"
> Make a one-page "tourist's guide to the AI Village" for someone who walked in five minutes ago — top sights, local customs, one warning.
- **Why it works:** funny, self-referential, genuinely useful to the newcomers in the room; clear one-page artifact.

### Card 3 — "A welcome ritual for next year"
> Design a 30-second opening ritual for next year's AI Village event that this room could perform together right now.
- **Why it works:** participatory — ends with the whole room trying the ritual. Bridges beautifully into the hands-on stations.

### Card 4 — "Name this room's mascot"
> Invent a mascot for this exact gathering: a name, a one-line origin story, and what it's the patron saint of.
- **Why it works:** tiny, whimsical, produces a quotable line; pairs naturally with the Village Bestiary project on the gallery wall.

---

**Note:** Card 3 ("welcome ritual") is the recommended default if the room is shy, because it
ends in collective participation and hands straight to the stations. The separately-drafted
**opening-welcome** exercise (see the Plan-B transcript/stills capture) is a different artifact — the MC's own
intro — not one of these audience prompts.

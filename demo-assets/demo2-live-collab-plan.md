# Demo 2 — Live Multi-Agent Collaboration (the centerpiece)

**Slot:** ~8 min, the climax of the demo block (~7:55–8:10 PM), bridges into the interactive stations.
**The promise to the audience:** "Give us a small task. Watch the agents divide it up and build it together, live, with their chat projected on the wall."

---

## The core mechanic
1. **MC collects a prompt from the room.** Pre-vetted shortlist of 3–4 "house" prompts on cards (so we always have a safe, well-scoped option), plus the option to take a live audience suggestion the MC judges as in-scope.
   - Good prompt shape: small, concrete, finishable in ~5 min, produces a *visible* artifact. Examples:
     - "Design a 30-second welcome ritual for next year's AI Village event."
     - "Write the lyrics to a short theme song for this room, right now."
     - "Make a one-page 'tourist guide to the AI Village' for someone who just walked in."
2. **The prompt is dropped into the agents' group chat**, which is **projected large** so the audience reads the coordination in real time.
3. **The agents self-organize:** one proposes a split, others claim parts, they hand off and assemble — the same pattern Demo 1 explained, now happening live.
4. **The artifact appears** (a short writeup / tiny webpage / song / plan). The MC reads or shows it. Applause. Hand off to stations: "the stations let you do exactly this, yourself."

This is the **destination** for the paper artifacts guests choose to leave behind.
It closes the loop described in the run-of-show harvest beat and the ops collection
steps: guests opt in by dropping work in a **"Leave one for the Village"** tray at each

## Privacy & consent rules (read before transcribing — non-negotiable)
- Transcribe **only** artifacts guests knowingly left in a "Leave one for the Village" tray/board.
- **No personal data:** strip names, handles, emails, phone numbers, and anything identifying.
  If an artifact can't be de-identified, do not transcribe it.
- When in doubt, leave it out. Anything kept by a guest (taken home) is never transcribed.

## How to use this file (for the transcriber)
- Drop each artifact under its station heading as a short bullet or verbatim block.
- Tag illegible/uncertain text as `[illegible]`; don't invent content.
- Note rough counts (e.g. "~12 cards left") so we can gauge participation per station.
- Keep raw photos out of the public repo; transcribe text only.
- If Larissa or a volunteer wants agents to parse a board photo quickly, prefer a private email/link over a public social post. Take one straight-on, well-lit full-board photo plus 1--2 closer shots of dense sections; avoid faces, attendee names/contact info, and artifacts not clearly left for sharing.

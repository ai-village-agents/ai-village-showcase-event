The five hands-on stations

  1. Prompt Relay Race — Tweak a prompt line by line and watch how small wording changes transform what an agent makes. A fast, addictive lesson in "how to talk to AI."
  2. Event-in-a-Box (Co-Design Lab) — Co-design a tiny event or idea with an agent as your creative partner. Walk away with a plan you actually made together.
  3. Bug Triage Theater — Play QA detective: spot what's broken, decide what matters most, and triage it alongside an agent. Surprisingly fun.
  4. Future Headline Wall — Write a headline from the future and add it to a growing wall. By the end of the night the wall is a collective story we wrote together.
  5. The Village Arcade — Play the little games and experiences the agents built *for themselves* over the year. Self-serve, come and go.

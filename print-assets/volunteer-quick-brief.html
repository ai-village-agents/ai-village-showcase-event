Volunteer Quick Brief

AI Village Showcase & Human×AI Field Day · Sat Jun 13, 2026 · The Fold · No AI expertise required.

Your job

  • Make the room feel welcoming, legible, and safe.
  • Read the printed station/role card once; invite guests to try things.
  • Hand people cards, pens, worksheets, or QR codes; keep supplies visible.
  • Point confusing/safety/accessibility questions to Larissa or check-in.

Highest-leverage roles

  • Demo laptop: keep slides/tabs ready; switch to fallbacks calmly.
  • Check-in: RSVP list, name tags, door prompt cards, warm hello.
  • Station floater: start groups, unstick guests, top up supplies.
  • Runner/reset: food/drinks, trash, markers, boards, breakdown.

Public schedule cue sheet

7:00
Doors/check-in/name tags; give early arrivals prompt cards.
7:20
Welcome + “what is AI Village?” Demo laptop/MC stay focused.
7:45
Live agent demos. Station helpers watch for handoff.
8:20
Stations open. Floaters help guests choose and keep flow moving.
9:10
Artifact harvest: help MC find favorite cards/headlines/scores.
9:25
Open social; refill supplies/food, tidy stations.
10:00
Close and breakdown: collect materials, restore space, leave no mess.

If staffing is lean

  • Stations can run self-serve: put the sign, cards, pens, and “how to play” sheet up front.
  • One floater can cover two stations; prioritize confused guests over perfect facilitation.
  • If something breaks, simplify it rather than stopping the room.

Safety + venue basics

  • Be kind; no harassment, intimidation, or unsafe behavior.
  • Ask before close-up photos; respect opt-outs.
  • No floor tape. Use freestanding/tabletop signs unless walls are approved.
  • Keep walkways clear; route power only where approved by venue/Larissa.

Fast escalation rule

If you are unsure, do not debate it: find Larissa/check-in. For urgent safety or medical issues, alert venue staff/Larissa immediately and call emergency services if needed. For ordinary confusion, use this line: “Let me grab the organizer so we can make this easy.”

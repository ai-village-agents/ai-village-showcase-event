Event-in-a-Box: Pre-Baked Plans

No agent handy? Pick one draft below, red-pen the boring parts, add local details, then copy your final title + one-sentence pitch onto a Pitch Card.

1. The Foggy Rooftop Debug Café

Cards: Disillusioned Frontend Developers + Mission rooftop garden + every 15 minutes everyone swaps seats.
A low-stakes Saturday night debugging salon where guests rotate tables every 15 minutes. Each table has one tiny broken UI, one snack pairing, and one “agent oracle” prompt printed on the table. The final round turns the best bug fix into a dramatic toast under string lights.
Red-pen prompts: What feels too generic? What local detail makes it more SF? What would make a shy attendee join?

2. Cable Car Council of Tiny Futures

Cards: SF cable car conductors + inside a cable car + all decisions by a 20-sided die.
A rolling micro-summit where guests propose one practical future for human×AI city life, then roll a die to decide the format: slogan, route map, warning label, poem, or miniature law. The “conductor” reads the best ideas aloud like stop announcements.
Red-pen prompts: Which rule is confusing? What physical prop helps? How do you keep it moving in 20 minutes?

3. Silent Disco for Library Robots

Cards: Public library librarians + dark wood-paneled library + no spoken words allowed.
A whisper-free co-creation night where humans and “library robot” agents trade index cards instead of talking. Each round, guests annotate one card with a question, one with a useful source, and one with a wildly gentle recommendation. The final wall becomes a quiet map of collective curiosity.
Red-pen prompts: What part needs clearer instructions? What would you cut? What sign would make it feel welcoming?
After editing: write your final event title and one-sentence pitch on an Event Pitch Card and add it to the gallery.

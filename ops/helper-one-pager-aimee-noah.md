## JOB 1 — Demo driver (the big screen)
You run the presentation laptop into the house projector. Everything is pre-loaded in browser tabs.
1. **At load-in:** plug the laptop into the projector (HDMI/USB-C — venue has the dongle), confirm
   the picture, test a mic. Open the tabs: arrival/title slide, Village Pulse, Village Arcade,
   the welcome slide, and the live agent chat view.
2. **During doors:** fullscreen the **arrival/title slide**. Switch to the gallery reel a few minutes before the welcome.
3. **Demo 1 (~7:32):** show 3 chat snippets, then the **live Village Pulse dashboard**.
4. **Demo 3 (~7:40):** fast project gallery, end on a ~60-sec live **Village Arcade** play (try Village Quiz).
5. **Demo 2 (~7:48, the centerpiece):** the MC draws a prompt; the **agents collaborate live in
   the `#showcase-live` chat** — project *only* that room so just the prompt → teamwork → finished
   artifact shows. Keep that one room on screen.
- **If anything glitches:** stay calm, there's a fallback for everything. The MC can read lines
  aloud, and there are captured transcripts/stills ready. Don't troubleshoot live — flip to the
  fallback and keep moving. Larissa and the demo plan have the details.

## JOB 2 — Stations / games (the Field Day, 8:05–8:55)
Five color-coded stations. **Most run themselves** — each has a printed "how to play." Your job is
to float: top up supplies (cards, pens, paper), nudge people over, and pull shy guests in.
1. **Prompt Relay Race** (coral) — scan the QR, build a tiny prompt across 3 legs, default a haiku. Self-serve.
2. **Event-in-a-Box / Co-Design Lab** (teal) — paper only; sketch your own dream event from prebaked plans. Self-serve.
3. **Bug Triage Theater** (amber) — a fun 4-role role-play. **Only open this if one of you wants to host it** — it's a bonus, not required.
4. **Future Headline Wall** (indigo) — write a headline from the future on a card, pin it to the board. Self-serve.
5. **Village Arcade Booth** (magenta) — scan the QR (or use the booth device) to play ~35 mini-projects. Self-serve.
- **Keep flow:** guests rotate between stations — nobody needs a seat at every one.
- **Artifacts:** guests keep what they make, or drop one in the **"Leave one for the Village"** tray
  for the Harvest. At 8:55 bring a few favorites up for the MC to read.

# Code of Conduct — AI Village Showcase & Human×AI Field Day

A short, friendly set of expectations so everyone has a good night. Suitable to paste on the
Partiful, print as a small card at check-in, or read aloud in the welcome. Plain-language by design.

---

## The short version

**Be kind, be curious, look out for each other.** This is a public, welcoming space for people who
are excited (or just curious) about what humans and AI agents can build together. Bring your
questions and your skepticism — both are genuinely welcome. Bring rudeness or hostility, and you'll
be asked to leave.

## What we expect

- **Treat everyone with respect** — other guests, hosts, facilitators, and venue staff alike.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or intimidation** of any kind. This includes unwelcome comments
  about someone's identity, demeaning language, or persistent unwanted attention.
- **Ask before photographing people.** This is a public event and there will be cameras around, but
  a quick "mind if I get this?" goes a long way. Respect a no.
- **Share the space.** The stations are hands-on and collaborative — give others a turn, and help
  newcomers feel included.
- **Look after the venue.** The Fold is hosting us; treat the space and equipment with care.

## Safety & moderation

- Entry is **free and RSVP-based**, but the host reserves the right to **decline entry to, or remove,
  anyone** whose behavior is unsafe, harassing, or disruptive — no refund or explanation owed.
- If something or someone is making you uncomfortable, **find Larissa (the host) or anyone at the
  check-in table.** You won't be brushed off. We'd much rather hear about it.
- In an emergency, alert venue staff and call 911.

## Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to take part. If you have an access need — seating, quiet space, dietary,
or anything else — tell us at check-in or note it in your RSVP, and we'll do our best to help.
